## 1. Purpose

This packet serves as the formal academic and pedagogical review instrument for native Mundari linguists and foundational education specialists. Its purpose is to evaluate 27 candidate bilingual records (8 narrative scenes and 19 atomic sentence pairs) translated into Mundari by **Jodheswar Barla** from the original Hindi story *"मछलियों की बारिश"* (authored by Ramendra Kumar, illustrated by Delwyn Remedios).

Under project governance rules, **no translation may be promoted to canonical status or bundled into classroom-facing tools without accredited human-in-the-loop validation**.

---

## 2. What the Human Reviewer Is Asked to Validate

Reviewers are asked to perform a rigorous dual-track evaluation:

1. **Linguistic Authenticity & Precision:**
   - **Semantic Equivalence:** Does the Mundari sentence faithfully convey the meaning of the Hindi source without misleading distortion?
   - **Morphosyntax:** Are agglutinative affixes, dual markers (`-kin`), plural markers (`-ko`), and verb-incorporated pronominal clitics natural and grammatically correct?
   - **Orthographic Consistency:** Is the Devanagari representation of Mundari phonology standard? In particular, review the use of the colon (`:`) indicating checked vowels / glottal stops (`ʔ`).
   - **Dialectal Register:** Identify whether the vocabulary aligns with Hasada (eastern / Khunti) or Naguri (western / Ranchi-Torpa) dialect conventions.
2. **Pedagogical & FLN Appropriateness:**
   - **Age Suitability:** Is the text appropriate for early childhood learners (Grades 1–3, ages 6–9)?
   - **Classroom Utility:** Can these phrases enrich teacher-led bilingual storytelling, vocabulary acquisition, and numeracy?
   - **Cultural Relevance:** Are indigenous cultural expressions (such as the bamboo basket `दाऊड़ा`) properly situated?

## 5. Review Instructions & Workflow

1. Review each record in **Section 8** sequentially.
2. Consult the automated **Machine Audit Flags** noting potential orthographic irregularities or alignment compressions.
3. Score each record against the **Linguistic Rubric (Section 6)** and **Educational Rubric (Section 7)**.
4. Assign an overall disposition:
   - **`ACCEPT`**: Linguistically authentic and educationally sound for classroom deployment.
   - **`REVISE`**: Semantically sound, but minor spelling, punctuation, or vocabulary corrections are required (detail in comments).
   - **`REJECT`**: Mistranslated, unnatural syntax, or inappropriate for early childhood education.
