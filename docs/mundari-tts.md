## 1. Educational Purpose & Scope

The **Mundari TTS Engine** provides pronunciation support for classroom flashcards, bilingual worksheets, and teacher guidance where certified native audio recordings are pending.

Pre-rendered educational audio covers:
1. **Grade 1 FLN Numerals 1–20**: From *मिअद* (1) through *बार हिसि* (20).
2. **Core Classroom Commands & Routines**: Greetings (*जोहार*), instructions (*दुबपे*, *तिंगुपे*, *आयुमेपे*, *किताब ओलोपे*), and positive reinforcement (*बेस कामी*).

Total pre-rendered assets: **36 verified 16 kHz WAV audio files**.
